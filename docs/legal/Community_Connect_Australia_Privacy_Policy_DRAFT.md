# Community Connect Australia Privacy Policy — consolidated pre-launch draft

Document version: `draft-2026-09-06-community-connect`

Prepared: 6 September 2026

Proposed effective date: the date this consolidated policy is published

Status: internal implementation draft; not published and not legal advice. It must be reviewed by Australian counsel before public launch.

## Operator and contact

Community Connect Australia is operated by Sajjad Zaheer in New South Wales, Australia. SIZA Apps is the product and initiative name.

- Website: https://siza.info
- Privacy and support email: support@siza.info

Contact `support@siza.info` with a privacy question, access or correction request, account-deletion request, security concern or complaint. We may need to verify the request before acting on it.

## Scope

This consolidated policy covers Community Connect Australia and both in-app modules: Community Events Australia and Community Businesses Australia. It applies to the Android and iOS apps, associated websites, administration tools and support channels.

## Information handled

- Account information: verified mobile number, name, optional email, role, default city, module preferences and legal-consent records.
- Event information: event and organiser details, posters, addresses, dates, speakers/reciters, stream links and event-management history.
- Directory information: private listing-owner/account data, optional ABN, declarations, exact address and contact routing; and the separately sanitised public business profile chosen by the owner.
- Communications: Contact Host, Contact Business, feedback, reports, support correspondence, replies and moderation history. Users must not include passwords, verification codes, financial details, sensitive identity documents or unnecessary personal information.
- Device information: notification permission and locally scheduled reminders, approximate/current location only when requested, calendar actions initiated by the user, app version, build and privacy-filtered diagnostics.

The app must not collect payment data in the initial release. Test builds accept fictional or specifically authorised test data only.

## Purposes

Information is used to operate accounts, publish user-submitted events/listings, route private messages, provide maps/nearby sorting/reminders/calendar/streaming functions, administer and moderate content, prevent misuse, send service and workflow email, respond to support requests, meet legal obligations and maintain app reliability. Information must not be sold.

## Business Directory separation

Private business submissions must remain in restricted records. Public directory reads use a sanitised projection that excludes owner UID, private contact routing, ABN number, declarations, moderation data and exact home/mobile-service addresses unless the owner explicitly chooses a public storefront address. ABN status is the only verification claim when an ABN is supplied and checked.

## Location, notifications and calendars

Location access is optional and requested in context. Users can choose a city without location access. Notifications and calendar access are controlled by device permissions and in-app preferences. Prayer reminders are scheduled locally and may be disabled in Profile & Settings.

## Disclosure and processors

Necessary data may be processed by contracted infrastructure and platform providers, including Google Firebase for authentication, database, storage, functions, notifications and crash diagnostics; Google Maps and Places for maps, geocoding and address suggestions; Amazon Web Services Simple Email Service (AWS SES) for service email; Expo/EAS, Apple and Google for app building, distribution and platform services; and YouTube and the configured video-streaming technology for authorised livestreams.

Service and workflow email is sent from `support@siza.info`, and replies are directed to the same monitored address. Some providers may process information outside Australia. The final policy must be checked against the production provider inventory and describe overseas processing where applicable.

## Retention, access and deletion

The service uses permanent, restricted archives for events, business listings, promotions, users, reports, moderation decisions and audit records. Account deletion disables authentication and marks the profile and calendar subscription inactive rather than erasing them. Public projections are removed when the underlying record is archived or marked deleted. Super Admin access is limited to safety, audit, restoration, dispute handling and repeat-submission eligibility review. Users may request access, correction, restriction and account closure through the documented process.

## Safety and complaints

Users may report content or contact `support@siza.info`. Authorised administrators may restrict or hide content while investigating. Reports and appeals must be handled consistently under the Directory Listing, Complaints and Takedown Rules.

## Children, security and changes

The final policy must state the intended age eligibility and guardian requirements. Reasonable technical and organisational safeguards include role-based access, public/private data separation, minimal crash metadata, audit records and restricted credentials. Material policy changes require a new version/effective date and renewed consent where legally required.

## Publication requirements

Before launch, obtain Australian legal review; confirm the operator disclosure, retention periods, age position, overseas-processing disclosure and complaint/regulator escalation path; and replace the current Events-only production page with this consolidated policy. Every app, PWA, store listing, user guide, support page and account-deletion page must use `support@siza.info`.
